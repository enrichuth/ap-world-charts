Atlantic Revolutions Comparison (Unit V)

American Revolution
1775–1783
Cause
Taxation without representation; Enlightenment ideas
Leaders
Washington, Jefferson, Franklin
Outcome
Constitutional republic; slavery preserved
Social change
Limited — white male property owners benefited most
Legacy
Model for later revolutions; Bill of Rights
French Revolution
1789–1799
Cause
Debt, famine, inequality, Enlightenment; American example
Leaders
Robespierre; Napoleon (aftermath)
Outcome
Republic → Reign of Terror → Napoleon
Social change
Abolished feudalism, nobility privilege
Legacy
Nationalism spreading across Europe; spread of revolutionary ideas
Haitian Revolution
1791–1804
Cause
Enslaved Africans inspired by French Revolution; brutal conditions
Leaders
Toussaint Louverture; Jean-Jacques Dessalines
Outcome
First Black republic; Napoleon gives up Haiti + Louisiana
Social change
MOST radical — abolished slavery; Black self-rule
Legacy
Terrified slaveholders worldwide; inspired abolition movements
Latin American Revolutions
1810–1825
Cause
Creole resentment; Napoleonic Wars destabilizing Spain
Leaders
Simón Bolívar, José de San Martín, Miguel Hidalgo
Outcome
Independence from Spain/Portugal; limited social change
Social change
Creoles replaced Spanish; Indigenous/enslaved still oppressed
Legacy
New nations; Monroe Doctrine; continued inequality

Quick Check Questions

Q2
How did the Mongol Empire both destroy AND facilitate trade? (Two examples of each.)
Q3
Compare how the Ottoman Millet system and the Mughal approach to diversity under Akbar differed.
Q4
Why is the Haitian Revolution considered the most radical of the Atlantic Revolutions?
Q5
Name THREE crops that moved from the Americas to the Old World via the Columbian Exchange. What effect did this have on global population?
Q6
Explain ONE way the Atlantic slave trade changed African political structures.
Q7
What is "contextualization" in an AP World essay, and how is it different from "causation"?
